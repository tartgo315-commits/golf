## 描述（4000 字以内，分段）

### 中文

**第一段 — 核心价值**  
GolfMate 是你的私人高尔夫伴侣：轻松记录每一场成绩，按世界差点系统（WHS）思路估算差点指数与趋势，让进步看得见。无论是 9 洞还是 18 洞，总杆、差杆与历史曲线一目了然，帮助你建立稳定的差点管理习惯。

**第二段 — AI 能力**  
内置 AI 复盘与赛前辅助：根据近期成绩与球场信息给出可执行的调整建议；结合训练计划，把「知道问题」变成「知道练什么」。赛前战术页帮助你在下场前理清策略重点。（说明：应用内差点与建议均为辅助计算，不构成官方 WHS 认证。）

**第三段 — 社交与记分**  
与好友对比差点趋势、查看公开资料；支持 Nassau 等常见记分与对局场景，让同组竞技更有趣。邀请码机制让你自主控制展示范围。

**第四段 — 数据与安全**  
成绩可配合锁定与同组验证等机制，降低事后争议风险。数据默认保存在本机，你可随时导出 JSON 备份；云端仅同步好友等功能所需的最小信息。我们不出售数据给广告商。

### English

**Paragraph 1 — Core value**  
GolfMate helps you log every round and track a WHS-style handicap index and trend so improvement is visible. Whether you play 9 or 18 holes, gross, differential, and history charts stay organized for consistent handicap management.

**Paragraph 2 — AI features**  
Get AI-assisted post-round review and pre-round guidance based on your recent scores and course context, plus structured training suggestions so you know what to practice next. Tactical views help you prepare before you tee off. (Handicap outputs are informational only and are not an official WHS certification.)

**Paragraph 3 — Social & scoring**  
Compare handicap trends with friends, share only what you choose, and enjoy common game formats including Nassau-style scoring for friendly competition.

**Paragraph 4 — Data & safety**  
Rounds can work with lock and peer verification flows to reduce disputes. Data stays on your device by default with optional JSON export; cloud sync keeps only the minimum needed for social features. We do not sell your data to advertisers.
